On-Road Price Breakup of Hyundai Ioniq 5 in Kolkata (2026)

The 2026 Hyundai Ioniq 5 in Kolkata starts from ₹58.80 Lakh and goes up to ₹58.94 Lakh on-road for the single fully-loaded Long Range RWD variant. Built on Hyundai's dedicated E-GMP electric platform, it blends lounge-like cabin space for Salt Lake and Park Street commutes with fast-charging capability for weekend drives on the Durgapur Expressway or NH-16.

Key Specifications & Charging Performance

  • Battery & Range: 84 kWh battery pack with up to 690 km claimed range for confident highway touring.
  • 18-Minute Fast Charge: Charges 10% to 80% in ~18 minutes on a 350 kW ultra-fast DC charger.
  • Platform & Drive: E-GMP skateboard architecture with Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD) automatic transmission.
  • Safety & Convenience: Level 2 ADAS, sliding rear seats, Vehicle-to-Load (V2L), and pixel LED lighting.

Kolkata & Metro Owner Perspectives

Lounge-Like Rear Space: Flat floor and sliding rear seats provide generous legroom for family chauffeured drives.
Quiet Highway Composure: Refined suspension absorbs rough patches cleanly while staying planted at expressway speeds.
City Regen Paddles: Paddle-controlled regenerative braking improves stop-and-go city efficiency and reduces brake wear.
Practical Considerations: Light-toned cabin upholstery needs regular upkeep, and its wide stance demands attention in narrow city lanes.

Frequently Asked Questions (Hyundai Ioniq 5 Kolkata)

What is the on-road price of the Hyundai Ioniq 5 in Kolkata?

The 2026 Hyundai Ioniq 5 on-road price in Kolkata ranges between ₹58.80 Lakh and ₹58.94 Lakh including WB EV registration, insurance, and TCS.

What is the ex-showroom price of the Hyundai Ioniq 5 in Kolkata?

Hyundai lists the 2026 Ioniq 5 ex-showroom price in Kolkata at ₹55,70,600.

How many variants are offered in India?

The 2026 Hyundai Ioniq 5 is sold in a single fully-loaded Long Range RWD automatic variant, so base and top-model pricing are identical.

What about pre-owned Hyundai Ioniq 5 options?

Certified pre-owned 2023–2024 72.6 kWh units typically trade around ₹28.75 Lakh to ₹40.00 Lakh depending on mileage and condition.
