BYD eMAX 7 Overview: Pricing & Positioning in India (2026)

Among the rapidly expanding lineup of EV cars in India, the BYD eMAX 7 stands out as a compelling 6/7 seater mpv from BYD. Priced between ₹26.90 – ₹29.90 Lakh (ex-showroom, India), it targets Indian buyers seeking zero-emission electric mobility without sacrificing everyday practicality. Built around a 55.4 kWh / 71.8 kWh Blade LFP BYD Blade LFP battery architecture, the BYD eMAX 7 pairs low per-kilometre running costs with modern connected technology and strong after-sales support across major Indian metros and Tier-2 cities.

Company Claimed Range vs Actual Real-World Range

One of the most critical questions for anyone researching EV cars in India is how far an electric vehicle actually travels on a single charge under Indian traffic and climate conditions. BYD officially advertises a claimed ARAI/MIDC/NEDC range of 530 km for the BYD eMAX 7. In independent real-world testing (Tested Highway/City Estimate (71.8 kWh)), the BYD eMAX 7 delivered an actual combined city-and-highway range of 385 km, achieving 73% of its laboratory-certified figure at a real-world energy efficiency of 5.36 km/kWh.

In city traffic with high regenerative braking, drivers achieve stronger efficiency than sustained highway cruising with summer AC load. Knowing the gap between claimed (530 km) and real-world range (385 km) helps plan intercity trips confidently.

Battery, Motor Performance & Charging Speeds

Powering the BYD eMAX 7 is a Permanent Magnet Synchronous (PMSM) driving the FWD layout, producing 201 bhp of peak output and 310 Nm of instant electric torque. Instant torque makes city overtaking effortless, with 8.6 sec (0-100 km/h) acceleration and a 180 km/h top speed.

Using the standard CCS2 connector compatible with Indian DC fast-charging networks (Tata Power EZ Charge, Jio-bp Pulse, Statiq), the BYD eMAX 7 fast-charges in 37 min (10-80% 115 kW DC), while an AC home wallbox completes a full charge in 10.5 hrs (7 kW AC).

Dimensions, Cabin Space, Safety & Color Options

Designed for Indian road conditions, the BYD eMAX 7 measures 4710 x 1810 x 1690 mm with a 2800 mm wheelbase and a healthy 170 mm ground clearance to tackle speed breakers and monsoon waterlogging confidently. Practicality is further bolstered by 6 or 7 seater cabin accommodation and 180 L (All rows up) / 580 L of luggage capacity. Key equipment highlights include 6-Seater Captain Chairs or 7-Seater Bench, 12.8-inch Rotating Touchscreen, Panoramic Glass Roof, Ventilated Front Seats.

Safety is anchored by 6 Airbags + Level 2 ADAS alongside 6 Airbags Standard, All 4 Disc Brakes, and an IP-rated battery enclosure backed by a 8 Years / 1,60,000 km (Blade Battery). Buyers can choose from 4 distinct exterior shades including Quartz Blue, Crystal White, Harbour Grey, Cosmos Black, allowing you to preview every color option directly in our interactive gallery above.

In India, the BYD eMAX 7 rivals the Kia Carens Clavis EV, Mahindra XEV 9S, BYD Atto 3. Choose it for 385 km real-world dependability and BYD service support, and compare variants on our EV Comparison Tool.

BYD eMAX 7 — Frequently Asked Questions

What is the real-world range of the BYD eMAX 7 in India?

While BYD claims 530 km (ARAI/MIDC/NEDC), the BYD eMAX 7 delivers an actual real-world range of around 385 km per full charge (5.36 km/kWh efficiency) in mixed city and highway driving.

What is the price of BYD eMAX 7 in India in 2026?

The BYD eMAX 7 is priced between ₹26.90 – ₹29.90 Lakh (ex-showroom, India).

How long does it take to charge the BYD eMAX 7?

Using a DC fast charger, the BYD eMAX 7 charges in 37 min (10-80% 115 kW DC). On a standard AC home wallbox charger, a full charge takes 10.5 hrs (7 kW AC).

What colors are available in the BYD eMAX 7?

The BYD eMAX 7 is offered in 4 exterior colors in India: Quartz Blue, Crystal White, Harbour Grey, Cosmos Black.
