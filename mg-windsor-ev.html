MG Windsor EV Overview: Pricing & Positioning in India (2026)

Among the rapidly expanding lineup of EV cars in India, the MG Windsor EV stands out as a compelling suv from MG. Priced between ₹14.10 – ₹19.15 Lakh (ex-showroom, India), it targets Indian buyers looking seeking zero-emission electric mobility without sacrificing everyday practicality. Additionally, MG offers a Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) ownership option (BaaS price from ₹9.99 Lakh) that lowers the upfront ex-showroom outlay for budget-conscious buyers. Built around a 38 kWh / 52.9 kWh (Pro) LFP Prismatic (IP67) battery architecture, the MG Windsor EV pairs low per-kilometre running costs with modern connected technology and strong after-sales support across major Indian metros and Tier-2 cities.

Company Claimed Range vs Actual Real-World Range

One of the most critical questions for anyone researching EV cars in India is how far an electric vehicle actually travels on a single charge under Indian traffic and climate conditions. MG officially advertises a claimed ARAI/MIDC/NEDC range of 449 km for the MG Windsor EV. In independent real-world testing (Autocar India Tested (38 kWh)), the MG Windsor EV delivered an actual combined city-and-highway range of 308 km, achieving 69% of its laboratory-certified figure at a real-world energy efficiency of 8.1 km/kWh.

In city traffic with high regenerative braking, drivers achieve stronger efficiency than sustained highway cruising with summer AC load. Knowing the gap between claimed (449 km) and real-world range (308 km) helps plan intercity trips confidently.

Battery, Motor Performance & Charging Speeds

Powering the MG Windsor EV is a Permanent Magnet Synchronous (PMSM) driving the FWD layout, producing 136 bhp of peak output and 200 Nm of instant electric torque. Instant torque makes city overtaking effortless, with 8.9 sec (0-100 km/h) acceleration and a 140 km/h top speed.

Using the standard CCS2 connector compatible with Indian DC fast-charging networks (Tata Power EZ Charge, Jio-bp Pulse, Statiq), the MG Windsor EV fast-charges in 45 min (10-80% DC), while an AC home wallbox completes a full charge in 6.5 hrs (7.4 kW AC).

Dimensions, Cabin Space, Safety & Color Options

Designed for Indian road conditions, the MG Windsor EV measures 4295 x 1850 x 1677 mm with a 2700 mm wheelbase and a healthy 186 mm ground clearance to tackle speed breakers and monsoon waterlogging confidently. Practicality is further bolstered by 5 seater cabin accommodation and 604 L of luggage capacity. Key equipment highlights include 135-Degree Aero-Lounge Rear Seats, 15.6-inch GrandView Touchscreen, Infinity View Glass Roof, 604 L Massive Boot Space.

Safety is anchored by 6 Airbags, 360 Camera alongside 6 Airbags, All 4 Disc Brakes, and an IP-rated battery enclosure backed by a Lifetime Battery Warranty (1st Owner) / 8 Years. Buyers can choose from 4 distinct exterior shades including Turquoise Green, Clay Beige, Pearl White, Starry Black, allowing you to preview every color option directly in our interactive gallery above.
