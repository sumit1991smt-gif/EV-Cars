VinFast VF7 Overview: Pricing & Positioning in India (2026)

Among the rapidly expanding lineup of EV cars in India, the VinFast VF7 stands out as a compelling suv from VinFast. Priced between ₹21.89 – ₹28.09 Lakh (ex-showroom, India), it targets Indian buyers looking seeking zero-emission electric mobility without sacrificing everyday practicality. Built around a 59.6 kWh / 70.8 kWh LFP Lithium-ion battery architecture, the VinFast VF7 pairs low per-kilometre running costs with modern connected technology and strong after-sales support across major Indian metros and Tier-2 cities.

Company Claimed Range vs Actual Real-World Range

One of the most critical questions for anyone researching EV cars in India is how far an electric vehicle actually travels on a single charge under Indian traffic and climate conditions. VinFast officially advertises a claimed ARAI/MIDC/NEDC range of 510 km for the VinFast VF7. In independent real-world testing (Autocar India Tested (70.8 kWh AWD)), the VinFast VF7 delivered an actual combined city-and-highway range of 391 km, achieving 77% of its laboratory-certified figure at a real-world energy efficiency of 5.51 km/kWh.

In city traffic with high regenerative braking, drivers achieve stronger efficiency than sustained highway cruising with summer AC load. Knowing the gap between claimed (510 km) and real-world range (391 km) helps plan intercity trips confidently.

Battery, Motor Performance & Charging Speeds

Powering the VinFast VF7 is a Dual PMSM (349 bhp AWD) driving the FWD / AWD Dual-Motor layout, producing 349 bhp of peak output and 500 Nm of instant electric torque. Instant torque makes city overtaking effortless, with 5.8 sec (0-100 km/h AWD) acceleration and a 175 km/h top speed.

Using the standard CCS2 connector compatible with Indian DC fast-charging networks (Tata Power EZ Charge, Jio-bp Pulse, Statiq), the VinFast VF7 fast-charges in 28 min (10-70% @ 110 kW DC), while an AC home wallbox completes a full charge in 7.5 hrs (11 kW AC).

Dimensions, Cabin Space, Safety & Color Options

Designed for Indian road conditions, the VinFast VF7 measures 4545 x 1890 x 1635 mm with a 2840 mm wheelbase and a healthy 190 mm ground clearance to tackle speed breakers and monsoon waterlogging confidently. Practicality is further bolstered by 5 seater cabin accommodation and 537 L of luggage capacity. Key equipment highlights include 12.9-inch Driver-Centric Display, 349 bhp Dual-Motor AWD Option, Level 2 ADAS + Head-Up Display, Panoramic Glass Roof.

Safety is anchored by 8 Airbags, ADAS L2 alongside 8 Airbags Standard, All 4 Disc Brakes, and an IP-rated battery enclosure backed by a 10 Years / 2,00,000 km (Vehicle & Battery). Buyers can choose from 4 distinct exterior shades including Crimson Red, Brahminy White, Neptune Grey, Jet Black, allowing you to preview every color option directly in our interactive gallery above.

Rivals & Who Should Buy the VinFast VF7?

In India, the VinFast VF7 rivals the Tata Harrier.ev, Mahindra XEV 9e, BYD Atto 3. Choose it for 391 km real-world dependability and VinFast service support, and compare variants on our EV Comparison Tool.
