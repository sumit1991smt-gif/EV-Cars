Everything We Know About the VinFast VF5 Electric SUV

A new electric SUV is getting ready to enter India's growing EV market! Vietnamese automaker VinFast is preparing to bring its compact VF5 electric SUV to India in 2027. With a fresh design, updated technology and a focus on affordable electric mobility, the upcoming model could attract buyers looking for a practical VinFast electric SUV without spending too much.

VinFast has confirmed plans to introduce the VF5 in India in 2027, with the launch expected in the first half of the year. According to Team-BHP and Autocar India, the company plans to adapt its existing global models for Indian conditions rather than develop an entirely new vehicle immediately. The VF5 will be positioned as an entry-level electric SUV, expanding VinFast's presence beyond its existing VF6 and VF7 models. The company has not yet announced the India-specific price or exact launch date. [Source: Team-BHP]

The VinFast VF5 E-SUV recently received a major update in international markets. The refreshed model gets a more upright SUV design, revised lighting, updated alloy wheels and a redesigned cabin. Inside, the dashboard now features a larger 10-inch touchscreen, replacing the earlier 8-inch display. The updated cabin also gets a new steering wheel design and a cleaner centre console. These changes could make the compact electric SUV more appealing to Indian buyers who value modern design and useful technology. [Source: Autocar India]

The global-spec facelift comes with a 43kWh battery pack and a front-mounted electric motor producing around 99hp and 135Nm of torque. It offers a claimed range of up to 370km on the NEDC testing cycle, along with support for 6.6kW AC charging and up to 60kW DC fast charging. These figures are for the international model; the final India-spec battery, power output and range may differ. [Source: Autocar India]

With its compact size and electric powertrain, the VF5 is expected to compete with models such as the Tata Nexon EV and Mahindra XUV 3XO EV. VinFast's local assembly facility in Tamil Nadu could also support its India expansion. However, pricing, localisation details and the final feature list are still awaited. The upcoming VF5 could become an important addition to India's affordable electric SUV segment if VinFast manages to offer competitive pricing and a reliable ownership experience.

Note: The 370km range is an international NEDC claim, not a confirmed Indian driving range. India-specific specifications and pricing are yet to be announced.
