Tata Harrier.ev 2026–2027 Review, BaaS Model & Buying Guide

BaaS Model Breakdown

Harrier.ev 65 kWh drops by ₹7.30 Lakh upfront under Tata's BaaS plan — from ₹21.79 Lakh outright down to ₹14.49 Lakh + ₹5.90/km battery rental (breakeven: ~1,23,729 km).

The flagship Tata Harrier.ev brings Quad Wheel Drive (QWD) dual-motor capability, a perfect 32/32 Adult 5-Star BNCAP score, and up to 627 km MIDC range. Outright pricing runs from ₹21.79 Lakh to ₹30.54 Lakh (CarDekho), while BaaS slashes entry upfront cost by ₹7.30 Lakh to ₹14.49 Lakh + ₹5.90/km.

65 kWh RWD vs 75 kWh RWD vs 75 kWh QWD Dual Motor

Choose Harrier.ev 65 (538 km MIDC, 420–445 km C75) for city and intercity comfort. Step up to Harrier.ev 75 RWD (627 km MIDC, 480–505 km C75) for maximum highway touring range, or QWD Dual Motor (504 Nm, 0–100 km/h in 6.3s, 6 terrain modes, 600 mm water wading) for all-terrain power.

Frequently Asked Questions (Tata Harrier.ev)

What is the BaaS price of Tata Harrier.ev?

Harrier.ev 65 kWh starts at ₹14.49 Lakh upfront + ₹5.90/km battery rental under BaaS, saving ₹7.30 Lakh over the ₹21.79 Lakh outright ex-showroom price.

What is the real-world range of Tata Harrier.ev?

Tata's internal C75 real-world range is 420–445 km (65 kWh), 480–505 km (75 kWh RWD), and 460–490 km (75 kWh QWD).

How fast does Harrier.ev QWD accelerate and charge?

With 504 Nm dual-motor torque in Boost Mode, Harrier.ev QWD hits 0–100 km/h in 6.3 seconds and charges 20%–80% in ~25 minutes on a 120 kW DC fast charger.

What is the Lifetime Battery Warranty on Harrier.ev?

First-registered private owners get Lifetime (15 years) unlimited-km HV battery warranty; second owners onwards get 10 years or 2 Lakh km from first registration.
