How to Use the EV Car Loan EMI Calculator

Planning your electric car budget in India is effortless with our EV Car Loan EMI Calculator. Follow three simple steps: first, enter or slide to choose your required Loan Amount (typically 80%–90% of the car’s on-road price after your down payment). Second, set the Rate of Interest (ROI) offered by your bank (many Indian banks offer green auto loans starting around 8.5%–9.5% p.a.). Third, pick your preferred Loan Tenure in years or months and click Calculate EMI.

Instantly, the calculator displays your fixed monthly EMI, total interest payable over the loan term, total amount paid, and a complete month-wise amortization schedule showing exact monthly interest, principal repayment, cumulative amount paid, and remaining principal balance.

Why Use an EV Car Loan EMI Calculator Before Buying?

Comparing monthly EMIs alongside monthly fuel savings reveals the true affordability of owning an electric car in India. For example, if upgrading to an EV adds ₹4,000 to your monthly EMI compared to a petrol car, but saves ₹7,000 per month on fuel and maintenance, your net monthly cash flow actually improves. Checking the month-wise principal balance also helps you plan part-prepayments or early loan closure to save lakhs in interest.

EV Car Loan EMI — Frequently Asked Questions

How is EV car loan EMI calculated in India?

EV car loan EMI is calculated using the standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1], where P is the loan principal, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the tenure in months.

Do Indian banks offer lower interest rates for EV car loans?

Yes, several public and private banks in India offer green car loan concessions of 0.10% to 0.50% lower interest rates compared to petrol/diesel auto loans.

What is the ideal tenure for an electric car loan?

Most Indian EV buyers choose a 5-year (60-month) or 7-year (84-month) loan tenure to balance affordable monthly EMIs against total interest outgo.

Can I include on-road price and charger cost in an EV car loan?

Many Indian banks finance up to 85%–90% of the vehicle's on-road price (ex-showroom plus insurance and registration), depending on your credit score.

Why should I check the month-wise amortization table?

The month-wise schedule shows exactly how much of each EMI goes toward principal vs interest and how much principal balance remains if you plan to foreclose early.
