2. VinFast VF5 2027 India Launch: What Changes vs Global Spec?

Tracked closely on our upcoming EV cars in India hub, the VinFast VF5 2027 will sit below the VF6 and VF7 as VinFast's entry-level compact e-SUV. Its recent global facelift adds a more upright stance, a larger 10-inch touchscreen (up from 8 inches), a new steering wheel, and a 43 kWh battery paired with a 99 hp / 135 Nm front motor. While VinFast offers battery subscription plans in select global markets, official India pricing and BaaS availability for the VF5 are yet to be announced.

3. 45 kWh vs 43 kWh Real-World Range & Charging Math

  • Tata Nexon.ev 45 kWh: With 489 km MIDC certification, real-world mixed driving comfortably delivers 350–375 km (~120–128 Wh/km). Plus, V2L & V2V support and a 40-minute (10–80%) 60 kW DC fast charge make highway runs predictable.
  • VinFast VF5 43 kWh: Rated at 370 km on the international NEDC cycle (optimistic vs Indian summer AC conditions), realistic city/highway range should fall around 270–300 km unless tuned further for India.

Tata Nexon EV BaaS vs VinFast VF5 2027 — FAQs

The Tata Nexon.ev 45 kWh BaaS ex-showroom price starts at ₹8.99 Lakh plus ₹4.4/km battery usage, bringing estimated New Delhi BaaS on-road pricing to roughly ₹9.55 Lakh before battery rental.
The VinFast VF5 is expected to launch in India in H1 2027. Its global facelift uses a 43 kWh battery with a 99 hp motor, 60 kW DC fast charging, and up to 370 km claimed NEDC range.
The Tata Nexon.ev 45 kWh delivers an estimated 350–375 km real-world range (489 km MIDC), whereas the global-spec VinFast VF5 43 kWh is rated up to 370 km NEDC (around 270–300 km real-world estimated).
BaaS lowers upfront cost by over ₹5.35 Lakh on the 45 kWh Creative variant, making it attractive for moderate monthly running. High-mileage drivers (1,500+ km/month) often save more over 5 years with full ownership.
VinFast offers battery subscription plans in select international markets, but official India pricing and whether BaaS will be offered on the 2027 VF5 have not yet been announced.
