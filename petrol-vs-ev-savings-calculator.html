How Electric Car vs Petrol Running Cost Works in India

Before buying an electric car in India, most buyers want to know how much money they will actually save compared to a petrol hatchback or SUV. Our Petrol vs EV Savings AI Calculator uses your real daily driving distance, your city's residential electricity tariff (or public DC fast-charging rate), and your EV battery's real-world efficiency to show exact per-km costs and annual savings.

For example, an electric SUV like the Tata Punch.ev (40 kWh) or Tata Nexon.ev (45 kWh) charged at home for ₹7.50/kWh costs roughly ₹0.90–₹0.95 per km, while a comparable petrol SUV typically costs ₹7.00–₹9.50 per km. Over 1,200 km of monthly driving, that translates into over ₹7,000+ saved every month—enough to offset a substantial share of your monthly EV car loan EMI.

Frequently Asked Questions (EV vs Petrol Savings)

1. How much does it cost to run an electric car per km in India? +

With home charging at ₹6–₹9 per unit (kWh) and real-world efficiency of 7.5–8.5 km/kWh, an electric car in India typically costs ₹0.80 to ₹1.20 per km—compared to ₹6 to ₹10 per km for a petrol car.

2. How is EV charging cost calculated for home charging vs fast charging? +

Full charge cost equals Battery Capacity (kWh) × Electricity Tariff (₹/kWh). For example, charging a 40 kWh battery at ₹7.5/kWh at home costs roughly ₹300, whereas public DC fast charging at ₹20/kWh costs around ₹800.

3. How much can I save per year by switching from petrol to an EV? +

Driving 40 km/day (1,200 km/month) in a compact EV charged at home typically saves ₹6,500–₹7,500 per month, or ₹75,000–₹90,000 per year in fuel costs alone.

4. Does public fast charging still save money compared to petrol? +

Yes. Even at ₹18–₹22/kWh on public DC fast chargers, an EV delivering 8 km/kWh costs roughly ₹2.25–₹2.75 per km, which is still less than half the fuel cost of most petrol SUVs.

5. Can EV fuel savings cover part of my monthly car loan EMI? +

Yes! You can take your monthly fuel savings from this calculator and test loan tenures in our EV Car Loan EMI Calculator to see how much of your EMI is offset by lower running costs.
