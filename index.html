Electric Cars in India: The Complete Guide to Buying an EV in 2026

Electric cars in India are no longer a niche idea. Passenger EV registrations rose 81.6% in the first half of 2026, reaching 151,050 units, and buyers now have more models, brands, and price points to choose from. Whether you commute across the city, drive a growing family, or want a premium ride, there is an EV that may suit your needs. This guide explains why electric cars in India are gaining popularity, which models and brands lead the market, and what to consider before you buy.

Why Electric Cars in India Are Gaining Momentum

Electric cars in India are attracting buyers for several reasons. Running costs are lower than petrol or diesel, since charging at home is usually cheaper than filling a tank. EVs also need less routine maintenance because they have fewer moving parts. Growing charging infrastructure, improving battery technology, and a wider range of models are helping more families make the switch. India's passenger EV penetration is still only about 4.4%, which means the market has plenty of room to grow.

Popular Electric Car Models in India

Several electric cars in India have become favourites among buyers:

  • Tata Nexon EV and Punch EV: Known for safety ratings, practical range, and a strong service network.
  • Mahindra XEV 9e and BE 6: Popular for bold design, space, and performance in the SUV segment.
  • MG Windsor EV: Offers comfort, modern features, and competitive pricing.
  • Maruti e Vitara: Maruti Suzuki entered the electric car market in January 2026 with this model, backed by one of the largest dealer networks in the country.
  • Hyundai Creta Electric: A familiar SUV experience in an electric form.

EV Cars in India — Frequently Asked Questions (7 FAQs)

Which are the best EV cars in India in 2026?

Top EV cars in India include Tata Tiago EV and MG Comet EV for city budgets, Tata Punch EV, Nexon EV, and MG Windsor EV under ₹20 lakh, and Mahindra BE 6 / XEV 9e for long-range travel.

What are the cheapest EV cars in India?

The Tata Tiago EV is the most affordable full-ownership EV starting at ₹6.99 lakh (ex-showroom), while MG Comet EV starts at ₹4.99 lakh under MG's Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) plan.

Which brand leads EV car sales in India?

Tata Motors leads Indian passenger EV sales with roughly 40.7% market share, followed by Mahindra (~23.7%) and MG Motor (~16.5%).

What EV cars under 15 lakhs are best for Indian roads?

Under ₹15 lakh, the Tata Punch EV, Tata Nexon EV base trims, MG Windsor EV (BaaS/base), and Citroen eC3 offer strong ground clearance and city-friendly range.

Are 7 seater EV cars available in India?

Yes, 7 seater EV cars in India include the Kia Carens Clavis EV, BYD eMAX 7, and Mahindra XEV 9S.

Which upcoming EV cars in India 2027 should buyers watch?

Key upcoming EV cars in India 2027 include the Hyundai Ioniq P EV (debut Jan 16, 2027), VinFast VF5 E-SUV, Tata Safari.ev, and Toyota Urban Cruiser Ebella.
