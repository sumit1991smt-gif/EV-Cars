EV Cars in India — Frequently Asked Questions

Which EV car in India gives the highest real-world range in 2026?

Among mass-market EV cars in India tested by Autocar India, the Mahindra XEV 9S (79 kWh) leads with 478 km real-world range, followed by the Mahindra XEV 9e (456 km) and Mahindra BE 6 (449 km). Flagships like Hyundai IONIQ 5 (510 km) and Kia EV9 (500 km) lead the luxury segment.

What is the cheapest EV car in India right now?

The Tata Tiago.ev is India's most affordable electric car with full battery ownership starting at ₹6.99 lakh (ex-showroom). Under MG's Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) plan, the MG Comet EV starts at ₹4.99 lakh + battery rental.

How many EV car brands are there in India in 2026?

There are 9 major mass-market and premium EV car brands in India listed on EV of India: Tata, Mahindra, MG, Hyundai, Maruti Suzuki, Kia, BYD, Citroen, and VinFast, spanning 28 electric car models.

Why is actual real-world range lower than company claimed range?

Company-claimed range is measured in controlled ARAI/MIDC/NEDC laboratory cycles without heavy AC load or high expressway speeds. Real-world Indian driving typically delivers 65% to 88% of claimed range depending on speed, AC usage, and regeneration.
