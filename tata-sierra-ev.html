Tata Sierra.ev 2026–2027 Review, BaaS Model & Buying Guide

BaaS Model Breakdown

Tata Sierra.ev 63 kWh starts at ₹11.99 Lakh upfront + ₹5.50/km under Tata's 2026 BaaS plan, saving ₹6.80 Lakh compared to the ₹18.79 Lakh outright ex-showroom price (breakeven: ~1,23,636 km).

The iconic Tata Sierra.ev returns on the Acti.ev+ architecture at ₹18.79 Lakh – ₹26.48 Lakh outright (CarDekho), or ₹11.99 Lakh + ₹5.50/km with Tata's BaaS program. With 665 km MIDC range (75 kWh RWD) and 510–530 km C75 real-world range, it is Tata's longest-range EV yet.

63 kWh vs 75 kWh vs QWD Dual Motor

The Sierra.ev 63 (Pure, Pure S, Adventure, Empowered) delivers 238 PS / 315 Nm and 566 km MIDC. The Sierra.ev 75 stretches MIDC range to 665 km, while the flagship Empowered A AWD (QWD) unleashes 504 Nm torque, 0–100 km/h in 5.8 seconds, 6 terrain modes, and 50%+ restart gradeability.

Horizon View Triple Screen, 622L Boot & BaaS Math

Sierra.ev stands out with its Horizon View Triple Screen (including passenger display), Panoramax sunroof, Boss Mode, 622L boot (expandable to 1,257L), and L2+ ADAS with 22 features. Check whether BaaS or full purchase saves you more in our BaaS vs Full Price AI Calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions (Tata Sierra.ev)

What is the BaaS price of Tata Sierra.ev in 2026?

Tata Sierra.ev 63 kWh starts at ₹11.99 Lakh upfront + ₹5.50/km battery rental under BaaS, saving ₹6.80 Lakh over the ₹18.79 Lakh outright price.

What is the real-world range of Tata Sierra.ev?

Per the official Sierra.ev brochure, C75 real-world range is 440–460 km (63 kWh), 510–530 km (75 kWh RWD), and 480–500 km (75 kWh QWD).

What are the variants of Tata Sierra.ev?

Sierra.ev is offered across Pure, Pure S, Adventure, Empowered, Empowered A, and Empowered A AWD.

How fast does Sierra.ev charge on a DC fast charger?

It charges 20%–80% in ~25–26 minutes on a 110–120 kW+ DC fast charger, adding up to 263 km of range in 15 minutes.
