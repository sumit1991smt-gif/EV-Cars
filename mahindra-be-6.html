2. How Mahindra BaaS Dual-Loan Works (Not Pay-Per-Km Rental)

  • Dual-Loan Ownership: Unlike pay-per-km battery leases, Mahindra BaaS splits financing into a ~5-year vehicle loan and a ~8-year battery loan. You legally own both the SUV and the battery pack.
  • Monthly Battery EMI: Battery EMI starts at ₹6,975/month (roughly ₹3.75/km benchmarked at 60 km/day driving).
  • Zero Minimum Km Lock-In: There is no mandatory monthly minimum kilometer rule—your battery EMI stays fixed whether you drive 400 km or 2,500 km a month.

4. Mahindra BE 6 Charging Time 0 to 80% & Home AC Options

Built on the INGLO platform, the BE 6 supports ultra-fast DC charging up to 140 kW (59 kWh), 160 kW (70 kWh), and 180 kW (79 kWh). Mahindra officially rates 20% to 80% in 20 minutes; from a depleted 0% to 80% state of charge, expect roughly 25–28 minutes on a 180 kW+ public charger depending on ambient temperature and battery pre-conditioning.

5. Mahindra BE 6 Safety Rating (Bharat NCAP) & Battery Warranty

  • 5-Star Bharat NCAP Rating: The Mahindra BE 6 achieved a 5-Star Bharat NCAP rating with 31.97 / 32 points in Adult Occupant Protection, paired with up to 7 airbags, 360-degree camera, blind-spot monitoring, and Level 2 ADAS.
  • Battery Warranty (Years & Km): Backed by a 10 Years / 2,00,000 km battery warranty. Even if you choose BaaS Dual-Loan financing, your factory battery warranty remains completely unchanged.
  • Dimensions & Practicality: Measuring 4,371 mm long with a 2,775 mm wheelbase, 207 mm ground clearance (222 mm at battery), 455L boot + 45L frunk, it easily handles rough Indian speed breakers.

6. Mahindra BE 6 Formula E Edition Features List & 3-Screen Interior Guide

The BE 6 Formula E (FE) and FE FOUR trims (79 kWh only, starting at ₹14.45 Lakh BaaS / ₹24.45 Lakh outright) add motorsport-inspired Firestorm Orange accents, Formula E badging, bespoke aero wheels, and 0–100 km/h performance in 6.7 seconds (210 kW / 285 PS, 380 Nm).

Inside, the Mahindra BE 6 interior 3-screen setup pairs a driver instrument display, a central infotainment touchscreen, and a dedicated co-driver entertainment display across the cockpit—letting you run navigation and ADAS telemetry on the driver cluster while the front passenger streams media independently.

Mahindra BE 6 BaaS & Specs — Frequently Asked Questions

The Mahindra BE 6 BaaS (Battery-as-a-Service) ex-showroom price starts at ₹11.45 Lakh for SPORTEQ ONE (59 kWh), going up to ₹16.95 Lakh for SPORTEQ FOUR / Launch Edition (79 kWh) and BE 6 FE FOUR. Outright ex-showroom prices without BaaS range from ₹19.45 Lakh to ₹26.95 Lakh.
On a compatible high-speed DC fast charger (140 kW for 59 kWh, 160 kW for 70 kWh, and 180 kW for 79 kWh), the Mahindra BE 6 charges from 20% to 80% in just 20 minutes, while a 0% to 80% DC fast charge takes approximately 25–28 minutes.
While MIDC claimed range is 573 km (59 kWh), 652 km (70 kWh), and 708 km (79 kWh), expect real-world highway range of 380–430 km (59 kWh), 435–475 km (70 kWh), and 480–530 km (79 kWh) at 90–100 km/h with AC on.
The Mahindra BE 6 holds a 5-Star Bharat NCAP safety rating with 31.97 out of 32 points in Adult Occupant Protection (AOP), backed by 6 to 7 airbags and Level 2 ADAS.
Mahindra offers a 10 Years / 2,00,000 km battery warranty on the BE 6 (including BaaS Dual-Loan buyers, where full factory warranty terms remain unchanged).
Unlike pay-per-km battery rentals, Mahindra BaaS is a Dual-Loan structure (~5 years vehicle loan + ~8 years battery loan, starting at ₹6,975/month or ~₹3.75/km benchmark at 60 km/day). You own both the car and the battery with zero minimum monthly km lock-in.
