Tata Tigor.ev 2026–2027 Review, BaaS Model & Buying Guide

BaaS Model Breakdown

Tigor.ev is sold under Tata's standard outright Ziptron pricing starting at ₹12.49 Lakh (ex-showroom). If you want lower upfront pricing with BaaS, compare Tigor.ev against Punch.ev BaaS (₹6.59L + ₹2.60/km) using our BaaS vs Full Price AI Calculator.

The Tata Tigor.ev is India's proven sub-4-metre electric sedan, starting at ₹12.49 Lakh (ex-showroom, CarWale). Backed by Tata's official Ziptron brochure specs, it pairs a 26 kWh IP67 liquid-cooled battery with a 55 kW (74.7 PS) / 170 Nm motor for 315 km ARAI range.

Practical Sedan Advantage: 316L Boot & 4-Level Regen

With 316 litres of enclosed sedan boot space, 5.1m turning radius, multi-mode 4-level regenerative braking, Cruise Control, and ZConnect smartwatch integration on XZ+ / XZ+ Lux, Tigor.ev shines for chauffeur-driven city users and airport/fleet commutes.

Variants & Smart Ownership Tools

Available across XE, XT, XZ+, and XZ+ Lux (Magnetic Red, Signature Teal Blue, Daytona Grey), top trims add a 17.78 cm HARMAN touchscreen, 8-speaker audio, projector headlamps, and rain-sensing wipers. Calculate your EMI on our EV Car Loan EMI Calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions (Tata Tigor.ev)

What is the official ARAI range of Tata Tigor.ev?

Per the official Tata Tigor.ev brochure, the 26 kWh battery delivers an ARAI certified range of 315 km, with ~230–250 km practical city range.

What is the boot space of Tata Tigor.ev?

Tigor.ev offers 316 litres of sedan boot space along with a 2,450 mm wheelbase.

How fast does Tata Tigor.ev charge?

It charges 10%–80% in 59 minutes on a DC fast charger and 10%–100% in 9.4 hours on a 15A socket or AC wallbox.

What warranty comes with Tata Tigor.ev?

Tata provides an 8 Years / 1,60,000 km warranty on the battery pack and motor, and 3 Years / 1,25,000 km on the vehicle.
