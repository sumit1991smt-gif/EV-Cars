Foldable Off-Grid Solar EV Charger: Gaotu's New Tech

Introduction

Imagine charging your electric car using sunlight, even when there is no electricity connection nearby. Sounds exciting, right? A Chinese company is working to make this possible with a new foldable off-grid solar EV charger. The technology combines foldable solar panels, battery storage and smart charging features, offering a new approach to electric vehicle charging in remote locations.

Gaotu Introduces Foldable Off-Grid Solar EV Charger

Chinese manufacturer Gaotu Innovation has introduced its GT-EV16-PB-FD solar charging system for the Australian market, according to pv magazine Global's report published on November 24, 2026.

The system is designed to be mounted on a vehicle's roof. It uses foldable photovoltaic (PV) panels to convert sunlight into electricity, allowing EV owners to generate power without relying on the electricity grid.

The manufacturer claims the system can provide up to 80 kilometres of daily driving range under suitable conditions.

Key Features of the Solar EV Charging System

The new portable solar EV charger combines several technologies in one system:

  • Solar power: The foldable solar array supports up to 1,600W of solar input.
  • Battery storage: A built-in 2.5kWh lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery stores energy for later use.
  • Solar efficiency: The manufacturer claims 25% cell-level conversion efficiency.
  • Smart charging: Dual independent MPPT trackers help manage solar energy collection.
  • Connectivity: Bluetooth and Wi-Fi support charging management through an app.

The system also offers off-grid EV charging backup power. Gaotu claims it can switch to backup power within 10 milliseconds during a mains power failure.

Why Is This Technology Interesting?

A foldable solar EV charger could be useful for camping, outdoor activities and travel through remote locations where charging stations are limited.

For Indian EV owners, this technology is particularly interesting because solar energy is widely available across the country. However, the product is currently introduced for Australia, and its availability and compatibility with Indian electric vehicles have not been confirmed.

The advertised driving range and performance will also depend on sunlight, vehicle efficiency and charging conditions.

A New Direction for EV Charging

Gaotu Innovation's foldable off-grid solar EV charger highlights how solar energy and battery storage could support electric mobility beyond conventional charging stations. The technology offers an interesting glimpse into the future of portable, renewable-energy-based EV charging.
