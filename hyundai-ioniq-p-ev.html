Hyundai's First Sub-4m Electric SUV for India

Hyundai is gearing up to enter India's competitive compact electric SUV market with a new model that could change the game for EV buyers. The upcoming Hyundai Ioniq P EV is set to make its debut on January 16, 2027, bringing a fresh design and modern technology to the mass-market electric car segment. With the Tata Nexon EV and other compact electric SUVs already attracting buyers, Hyundai's new offering could make the competition even more interesting.

The Hyundai Ioniq P EV will be the brand's first sub-4-metre electric SUV developed specifically for India. It is expected to use Hyundai's E-GMP (K) electric vehicle platform, which also underpins the international-spec Inster EV. The new SUV has been spotted testing on Indian roads, and its design is expected to feature a boxy silhouette, short overhangs, LED lighting and an upright SUV stance. Hyundai has not officially confirmed the final name or complete design details yet. [Source: Autocar India]

Battery Versions, ADAS & Next-Gen Cabin Tech

The upcoming electric SUV is expected to be offered in Standard Range and Long Range versions. However, Hyundai has not revealed the battery capacity, motor output, charging speed or certified driving range for the India-spec model. The company has confirmed that the new EV will feature Level 2 ADAS and a next-generation infotainment system. The latter could be the Pleos Connect interface, although this is yet to be officially confirmed. These features could help the Ioniq P stand out in a segment where technology and everyday practicality matter greatly. [Source: Team-BHP]

Expected Price Reveal, Local Manufacturing & Rivals

Hyundai is expected to announce the Ioniq P EV's price at the Bharat Mobility Global Expo 2027, scheduled from February 4 to 9. The electric SUV will be manufactured at Hyundai's Sriperumbudur facility in Tamil Nadu and is expected to compete with the Tata Nexon EV, Mahindra XUV 3XO EV and Kia Syros EV. With local manufacturing and a compact footprint, the Hyundai Ioniq P EV could become an important addition to India's growing electric vehicle market. More details on pricing, battery options and specifications are expected closer to its debut. [Source: Auto Expo 2027 Report]
