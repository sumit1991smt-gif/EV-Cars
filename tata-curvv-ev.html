Tata Curvv.ev SeriesX 2026–2027 Review, BaaS Model & Buying Guide

BaaS Model Breakdown

With Tata's 2026 BaaS plan, Curvv.ev SeriesX 55 starts at ₹10.99 Lakh upfront + ₹5.00/km battery rental, cutting ₹6.20 Lakh off the ₹17.19 Lakh outright price (breakeven: 1,24,000 km).

India's first electric coupé-SUV, the Tata Curvv.ev SeriesX, brings 5-Star Bharat NCAP safety (30.81/32 AOP, 44.83/49 COP) and a 55 kWh battery pack at ₹17.19 Lakh – ₹19.69 Lakh outright (CarDekho) or ₹10.99 Lakh + ₹5.00/km under Tata's BaaS program.

SeriesX 55 kWh Performance, Range & Bi-Directional Tech

Per the official SeriesX brochure, the Gen-2 123 kW (167 PS) / 215 Nm motor hits 0–100 km/h in 8.6 seconds and delivers 502 km MIDC (400–425 km C75 real-world). It also includes 70 kW+ DC fast charging (10%–80% in 40 mins), a 500L boot + 11.6L frunk, 5 kVA V2V, and 3.3 kVA V2L.

Accomplished X 55 vs Empowered X 55

Accomplished X 55 packs a panoramic sunroof, 10.25" Harman screen, 360° 3D camera, and R17 aero alloys. Stepping up to Empowered X 55 adds a 12.3" Harman screen, 9-speaker JBL subwoofer audio, ventilated front seats, powered gesture tailgate, R18 alloys, and full Level 2 ADAS. Frequently Asked Questions (Tata Curvv.ev SeriesX)

What is the BaaS price of Tata Curvv.ev in 2026?

Tata Curvv.ev 55 kWh starts at ₹10.99 Lakh upfront + ₹5.00/km battery rental under BaaS, saving ₹6.20 Lakh compared to the ₹17.19 Lakh outright price.

What is the real-world range of Curvv.ev SeriesX 55?

Tata's C75 real-world estimate is 400–425 km on a full charge against the 502 km MIDC rating.

Does Curvv.ev SeriesX come with a lifetime battery warranty?

Yes, Tata offers a Lifetime HV Battery Warranty (15 years / unlimited km) for first-registered private owners, and 8 years / 1.6 Lakh km for subsequent owners.

What is the boot space of Tata Curvv.ev?

Curvv.ev provides 500 litres of rear boot space plus an 11.6-litre front frunk.
