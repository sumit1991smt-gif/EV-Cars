Tata Nexon EV 2027 Review, BaaS & Buying Guide

The Tata Nexon EV remains India's benchmark compact electric SUV heading into 2027, blending 5-star Bharat NCAP safety, punchy performance, and an upgraded 45 kWh battery pack. Whether you are upgrading from the older Nexon EV Max or buying your first electric car, this guide covers the latest pricing, BaaS option, range, and interior updates.

Tata Nexon EV Price & City-Wise On-Road Price

The Tata Nexon EV price starts at ₹12.49 Lakh (ex-showroom) for the Creative Plus MR (30 kWh) and goes up to ₹17.89 Lakh for the top Empowered+ A 45 Red Dark Edition. Checking the Nexon EV on road price across major cities, base trims start from roughly ₹13.22 Lakh in Delhi, ₹13.21 Lakh in Jaipur, ₹13.37 Lakh in Hyderabad, ₹13.27 Lakh in Bhubaneswar, and ₹14.60 Lakh in Bangalore, depending on state EV road-tax exemptions and insurance.

Tata Nexon EV BaaS Model Explained

To make long-range electric driving even more accessible, Tata Motors added the 45 kWh Nexon.ev to its Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) programme. Under BaaS, the 45 kWh version starts at just ₹8.99 Lakh (ex-showroom) with battery financing quoted at ₹4.4 per km. By separating the battery cost from the vehicle chassis, buyers enjoy a significantly lower upfront down payment and smaller monthly car loan EMI—ideal for urban families who want 45 kWh range without stretching their initial budget.

Tata Nexon EV Range, Mileage & Battery Warranty

Buyers comparing Tata Nexon EV range and real-world Tata Nexon EV mileage per charge have two battery choices. The 30 kWh Medium Range (MR) delivers 275 km certified (210–230 km real-world), while the 45 kWh pack pushes certified range to 489 km (350–375 km real-world) and sprints from 0–100 km/h in 8.9 seconds. With 60 kW DC fast charging, 10–80% takes just 40 minutes. Best of all, the Nexon EV battery warranty on the 45 kWh pack offers Lifetime & Unlimited km coverage for the first private owner (8 years / 1,60,000 km on 30 kWh and subsequent owners).

Tata Nexon EV Interior, V2L Tech & Variants

Step inside the Tata Nexon EV interior and you are greeted by a crisp 12.3-inch Harman infotainment touchscreen, a 10.25-inch digital cockpit, front ventilated leatherette seats, a panoramic sunroof, a 9-speaker JBL sound system, and Level-1 ADAS on top trims. Practical additions like V2L (Vehicle-to-Load) and V2V charging let you power camping appliances or rescue another EV on road trips, while steering-mounted paddle shifters offer 4-level regenerative braking.

Nexon EV Max vs. Nexon.ev 45: Which Variant Should You Buy?

Replacing the older 40.5 kWh Nexon EV Max, the newer 45 kWh lineup brings faster 60 kW DC charging, a higher 489 km MIDC range, and 350 litres of boot space with up to 205 mm ground clearance (30 kWh). City commuters can save with Creative Plus MR (₹12.49 Lakh), while families planning regular highway drives will find Fearless 45 (₹15.34 Lakh) and Empowered 45 (₹16.34 Lakh) deliver the strongest all-round value for 2027 buyers.

Frequently Asked Questions (Tata Nexon EV)

1. What is the Tata Nexon EV price and BaaS price in India? +

The Tata Nexon EV ex-showroom price starts at ₹12.49 Lakh for the Creative Plus MR (30 kWh) and goes up to ₹17.69–₹17.89 Lakh for the Empowered+ A 45 and Red Dark Edition. Under Tata's Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) programme, the 45 kWh version starts at ₹8.99 Lakh with battery financing quoted at ₹4.4 per km.

2. What is the Tata Nexon EV on road price in Jaipur, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Bhubaneswar, and Delhi? +

Depending on state EV road-tax policies and insurance, the Tata Nexon EV on-road price starts from roughly ₹13.22 Lakh in New Delhi, ₹13.21 Lakh in Jaipur, ₹13.37 Lakh in Hyderabad, ₹13.27 Lakh in Bhubaneswar, and ₹14.60 Lakh in Bangalore.

3. What is the real-world range and mileage of the Tata Nexon EV 45 vs 30 kWh? +

The 45 kWh Nexon.ev offers an MIDC-certified range of 489 km (estimated 350–375 km real-world), while the 30 kWh Medium Range (MR) delivers 275 km certified (210–230 km real-world).

4. What is the Tata Nexon EV battery warranty? +

The Nexon.ev 45 kWh variants come with a Lifetime HV Battery Warranty (15 years per the Motor Vehicles Act) with unlimited km for the first private owner, and 8 years / 1,60,000 km for subsequent owners. The 30 kWh MR carries an 8-year / 1,60,000 km warranty.

5. How is the new Nexon.ev 45 different from the older Nexon EV Max? +

Replacing the earlier Nexon EV Max (40.5 kWh), the newer Nexon.ev 45 packs a larger 45 kWh battery with up to 489 km MIDC range, faster 60 kW DC charging (10–80% in 40 mins), panoramic sunroof, V2L/V2V, and Level-1 ADAS on top trims.

6. What are the interior highlights of the Tata Nexon EV? +

Top trims feature a 12.3-inch Harman touchscreen, 10.25-inch digital driver display, front ventilated leatherette seats, panoramic sunroof, JBL 9-speaker audio, 360-degree camera, wireless charger, and 350-litre boot space.

7. How long does the Tata Nexon EV take to charge? +

On a 60 kW DC fast charger, the 45 kWh pack charges from 10% to 80% in 40 minutes (56 minutes on a 50 kW DC charger for the 30 kWh pack). A 7.2 kW AC home wall-box takes up to 6 hours 36 minutes for a 10–100% charge.
