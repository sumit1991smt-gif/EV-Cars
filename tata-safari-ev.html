1. Battery Capacity & Pack Math: 65 kWh vs 75 kWh

With the Tata Safari EV spied testing in India ahead of a tentative December 2026 – January 2027 launch window, families considering a three-row electric SUV want realistic range numbers rather than lab-cycle claims. Based on the Tata.ev acti.ev+ architecture proven on the Tata Harrier.ev, industry projections point to 65 kWh and 75 kWh battery packs:

2. Triple-Screen Dashboard, 6-Seater vs 7-Seater & 50 kW Charging

  • Triple-Screen Dashboard: Spy shots reveal a wide three-screen layout (digital cockpit, central infotainment, and co-driver passenger display), alongside expected Level 2 ADAS and V2L/V2V support.
  • 6-Seater vs 7-Seater: Expected in both 6-seat (2nd-row captain chairs with walk-through access) and 7-seat (2nd-row bench) layouts, with 3rd-row seats folding flat for weekend luggage.
  • City vs Highway Shift: Regenerative braking and lower drag keep city range higher, while sustained 90–100 km/h highway cruising with AC increases Wh/km draw.
  • 50 kW & 120 kW+ DC Charging: A 50 kW DC charger takes ~55–75 mins (10–80%), while 120 kW+ ultra-fast chargers (as on Harrier.ev) cut stops to ~25–30 mins.

Frequently Asked Questions (Tata Safari EV)

What is the expected real world range of Tata Safari EV?

Depending on the expected 65 kWh or 75 kWh battery pack, real-world driving is projected at 390–490 km in city traffic and 310–395 km on highways.

Does Tata Safari EV get a triple screen dashboard?

Yes, spotted test mules show a wide triple-screen dashboard layout combining the digital cluster, central touchscreen, and co-driver screen.

Is Tata Safari EV a 6 seater or 7 seater?

It is expected in both 6-seater (captain chairs) and 7-seater (bench seat) three-row layouts, pending official launch specs.

What is the expected Tata Safari EV launch date and price in India?

Expected around December 2026 – January 2027 at an estimated ₹26.00 Lakh – ₹30.00 Lakh ex-showroom.

How does Safari EV differ from Harrier EV and BYD eMAX 7?

Safari EV adds a 3-row 6/7-seater cabin over the 5-seater Harrier EV, and brings SUV ground clearance and expected QWD AWD compared to the BYD eMAX 7 MPV.
