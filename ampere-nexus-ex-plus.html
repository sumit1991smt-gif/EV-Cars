Ampere Nexus EX Plus Review: Why LFP Chemistry & Mid-Mount Motor Matter

The Ampere Nexus EX Plus (from Greaves Electric Mobility) is engineered specifically for Indian family and daily commuter conditions at ₹1,29,999 ex-showroom (Ampere Official). Unlike many rivals that rely on NMC cells, Ampere equips the Nexus EX+ with a 3 kWh LFP battery rated for up to 2,00,000 km of cycle life and higher thermal stability in 45°C Indian summers (EVFY).

Mid-Mount 5 kW Motor & Aeronautics-Grade Steel Chassis

Moving from a hub motor to a 5 kW peak mid-mount motor (202 Nm wheel torque) gives balanced weight distribution, a 93 km/h top speed, and 18° gradeability for steep flyovers even with a pillion. Its SPFH 590 dual-frame steel chassis supports 200 kg load with 170 mm ground clearance and a flat 263 mm floorboard.

Running Cost vs Petrol Scooter

A full 3 kWh home charge at ₹7/kWh costs roughly ₹21 for ~105 km of real-world riding (~₹0.20/km) versus ~₹2.20/km on a 45 km/L petrol scooter. Check your exact monthly savings in our Petrol vs EV Savings AI Calculator or plan monthly EMIs in the EV Loan EMI Calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions (Ampere Nexus EX Plus)

What is the ex-showroom and on-road price of Ampere Nexus EX Plus?

Ampere Nexus EX+ costs ₹1,29,999 ex-showroom (₹1,35,999 with Intellipack). On-road prices start around ₹1.36 Lakh in Delhi and Jaipur.

What is the real-world range of Ampere Nexus EX Plus?

Certified IDC range is 136 km; in practical city traffic with Eco/City mode, riders get ~100–115 km per full charge.

What is included in the EX+ Intellipack variant?

EX+ Intellipack (₹1,35,999) adds 30+ connected app features via Ampere Connect including turn-by-turn GPS navigation, geo-fencing, and Find My Scooter.

How long does it take to charge?

With the included IP65 water-resistant 750W charger, 20% to 80% takes 2 hours 30 minutes.
