Kia EV9 Overview: Pricing & Positioning in India (2026)

Among the rapidly expanding lineup of EV cars in India, the Kia EV9 stands out as a compelling suv from Kia. Priced between ₹130.00 Lakh (ex-showroom, India), it targets Indian buyers looking seeking zero-emission electric mobility without sacrificing everyday practicality. Built around a 99.8 kWh NMC 800V E-GMP Platform battery architecture, the Kia EV9 pairs low per-kilometre running costs with modern connected technology and strong after-sales support across major Indian metros and Tier-2 cities.

Company Claimed Range vs Actual Real-World Range

One of the most critical questions for anyone researching EV cars in India is how far an electric vehicle actually travels on a single charge under Indian traffic and climate conditions. Kia officially advertises a claimed ARAI/MIDC/NEDC range of 561 km for the Kia EV9. In independent real-world testing (Estimated Real-World (99.8 kWh AWD)), the Kia EV9 delivered an actual combined city-and-highway range of 500 km, achieving 89% of its laboratory-certified figure at a real-world energy efficiency of 5.01 km/kWh.

In city traffic with high regenerative braking, drivers achieve stronger efficiency than sustained highway cruising with summer AC load. Knowing the gap between claimed (561 km) and real-world range (500 km) helps plan intercity trips confidently.

Battery, Motor Performance & Charging Speeds

Powering the Kia EV9 is a Dual PMSM (384 PS) driving the AWD Dual-Motor layout, producing 379 bhp of peak output and 700 Nm of instant electric torque. Instant torque makes city overtaking effortless, with 5.3 sec (0-100 km/h) acceleration and a 200 km/h top speed.

Using the standard CCS2 (350 kW DC) connector compatible with Indian DC fast-charging networks (Tata Power EZ Charge, Jio-bp Pulse, Statiq), the Kia EV9 fast-charges in 24 min (10-80% @ 350 kW DC), while an AC home wallbox completes a full charge in 9.0 hrs (10-100% @ 11 kW AC).

Dimensions, Cabin Space, Safety & Color Options

Designed for Indian road conditions, the Kia EV9 measures 5015 x 1980 x 1780 mm with a 3100 mm wheelbase and a healthy 198 mm ground clearance to tackle speed breakers and monsoon waterlogging confidently. Practicality is further bolstered by 6 seater cabin accommodation and 333 L + 52 L Frunk of luggage capacity. Key equipment highlights include Flagship 6-Seater Electric Luxury SUV, 2nd Row Captain Seats with Massage & Ottoman, 10 Airbags + Level 2 ADAS, 384 PS / 700 Nm Dual-Motor AWD.

Safety is anchored by 10 Airbags, 5-Star Euro NCAP, ADAS L2 alongside 10 Airbags Standard, All 4 Ventilated Discs, and an IP-rated battery enclosure backed by a 8 Years / 1,60,000 km (Battery). Buyers can choose from 4 distinct exterior shades including Ocean Blue, Snow White Pearl, Panthera Metal, Aurora Black Pearl, allowing you to preview every color option directly in our interactive gallery above.

What is the real-world range of the Kia EV9 in India?
