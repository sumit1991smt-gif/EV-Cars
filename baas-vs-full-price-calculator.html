How to Use the BaaS Model vs Full Price AI Calculator

Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) splits the cost of an electric car into two parts: you buy the vehicle chassis at a sharply reduced upfront price and pay a per-kilometre battery subscription to a finance partner. Follow these four steps to test whether BaaS or full outright ownership fits your driving budget:

  1. Step 1: Pick a Model Preset or Custom EVTap a preset like Tata Punch.ev, Tata Nexon.ev 45, MG Windsor EV, or MG Comet EV to auto-fill verified ex-showroom prices and per-km battery rates, or enter quotes from your local dealer.
  2. Step 2: Set Your Realistic Monthly DistanceSlide your average monthly driving (for example, 800 km/month for office commutes or 2,200 km/month for heavy highway/fleet use).
  3. Step 3: Adjust Loan & Ownership HorizonEnter your down payment percentage, bank EV loan interest rate, and how many years you plan to keep the car (5 years is the typical Indian upgrade cycle).
  4. Step 4: Read the Breakeven & Total CostIf your total driving over your ownership period stays below the Breakeven Distance, BaaS keeps more cash in your pocket. Above it, Full Ownership wins.

Frequently Asked Questions (BaaS vs Full EV Price)

What is BaaS (Battery-as-a-Service) in Indian EVs?

BaaS separates the battery price from the car price. You pay a lower ex-showroom amount for the vehicle and pay a per-km battery rental fee (for example ₹2.60/km to ₹4.50/km) to the BaaS finance partner.

How do I calculate the BaaS breakeven distance?

Subtract the BaaS upfront price from the Full Ownership ex-showroom price, then divide by the ₹/km battery rental rate. Above that total kilometre mark, Full Ownership becomes cheaper.

Is electricity charging included in the BaaS ₹/km fee?

No. The BaaS fee covers battery leasing only. You still pay for home or public fast charging separately — check your exact charging cost using our Petrol vs EV Savings AI Calculator.

Are there minimum monthly km clauses in BaaS contracts?

Yes, several BaaS finance partners specify a minimum monthly billing threshold (such as ~1,500 km/month on select plans). Always confirm whether unused kilometres roll over or carry a minimum monthly charge.

Does BaaS reduce RTO road tax and insurance?

Because the vehicle invoice value is lower under BaaS, state registration charges (where applicable on EVs) and initial IDV-linked insurance premiums can be lower than full-price variants.

Can I sell a BaaS EV later?

Yes, you can either foreclose/buy out the remaining battery balance or transfer the active BaaS battery subscription to the next buyer subject to financier approval.
