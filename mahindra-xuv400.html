Mahindra XUV400 EV Overview: Pricing & Positioning in India (2026)

Among the rapidly expanding lineup of EV cars in India, the Mahindra XUV400 EV stands out as a compelling suv from Mahindra. Priced between ₹15.49 – ₹17.69 Lakh (ex-showroom, India), it targets Indian buyers looking seeking zero-emission electric mobility without sacrificing everyday practicality. Built around a 34.5 kWh / 39.4 kWh NMC Lithium-ion (IP67) battery architecture, the Mahindra XUV400 EV pairs low per-kilometre running costs with modern connected technology and strong after-sales support across major Indian metros and Tier-2 cities.

Company Claimed Range vs Actual Real-World Range

One of the most critical questions for anyone researching EV cars in India is how far an electric vehicle actually travels on a single charge under Indian traffic and climate conditions. Mahindra officially advertises a claimed ARAI/MIDC/NEDC range of 456 km for the Mahindra XUV400 EV. In independent real-world testing (CarDekho Tested (39.4 kWh)), the Mahindra XUV400 EV delivered an actual combined city-and-highway range of 290 km, achieving 64% of its laboratory-certified figure at a real-world energy efficiency of 7.3 km/kWh.

In city traffic with high regenerative braking, drivers achieve stronger efficiency than sustained highway cruising with summer AC load. Knowing the gap between claimed (456 km) and real-world range (290 km) helps plan intercity trips confidently.

Battery, Motor Performance & Charging Speeds

Powering the Mahindra XUV400 EV is a Permanent Magnet Synchronous (PMSM) driving the FWD layout, producing 148 bhp of peak output and 310 Nm of instant electric torque. Instant torque makes city overtaking effortless, with 8.3 sec (0-100 km/h) acceleration and a 150 km/h top speed.

Using the standard CCS2 connector compatible with Indian DC fast-charging networks (Tata Power EZ Charge, Jio-bp Pulse, Statiq), the Mahindra XUV400 EV fast-charges in 50 min (0-80% @ 50 kW DC), while an AC home wallbox completes a full charge in 6h 30m (7.2 kW AC).

Dimensions, Cabin Space, Safety & Color Options

Designed for Indian road conditions, the Mahindra XUV400 EV measures 4200 x 1821 x 1634 mm with a 2600 mm wheelbase and a healthy 200 mm ground clearance to tackle speed breakers and monsoon waterlogging confidently. Practicality is further bolstered by 5 seater cabin accommodation and 378 L of luggage capacity. Key equipment highlights include 10.25-inch Touchscreen + Digital Cluster, Dual-Zone Climate Control, Electric Sunroof, Adrenox Connected Car Tech.

Safety is anchored by 6 Airbags, ESP alongside 6 Airbags, All 4 Disc Brakes, and an IP-rated battery enclosure backed by a 8 Years / 1,60,000 km (Battery) | 3 Years / Unlimited km. Buyers can choose from 4 distinct exterior shades including Arctic Blue, Everest White, Galaxy Grey, Napoli Black, allowing you to preview every color option directly in our interactive gallery above.
